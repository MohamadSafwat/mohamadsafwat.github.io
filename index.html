HOW A DIAGNOSTIC RUNS

01

Twelve months of purchases, sales and stock — exported or read-only. No new data collection.

02

Find the leaks

Price variance, supplier concentration, discount leakage, fulfilment failure, slow stock, costs ahead of sales.

03

Separate symptom from cause

Most root causes are process problems — a missing number at the moment of a decision.

04

Deliver and hand over

Analysis, executive deck, a prioritised change list — and tools your team runs without me.
